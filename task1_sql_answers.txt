Question 3: What are the most popular books?

SQL:
SELECT b.title, COUNT(c.checkout_id) AS count
FROM books b
JOIN checkouts c
ON b.book_id = c.book_id
GROUP BY b.title
ORDER BY count DESC
LIMIT 5 
Result:
